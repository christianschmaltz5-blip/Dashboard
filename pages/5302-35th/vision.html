The Site

A fenced, vacant infill parcel — with a small existing structure.

5302 N 35th Ave sits on the west side of 35th Ave between W Vermont Ave and W Georgia Ave, in the Maryvale / Alhambra area of Phoenix — a block from Grand Canyon University and surrounded by established low-rise apartment and single-family blocks.

The Vision

Spanish Mediterranean, built around a courtyard.

Stucco walls, clay-tile rooflines, arched entries and wrought-iron balconies wrap a shared pool courtyard — the same infill multifamily language used across recent Phoenix redevelopments.
